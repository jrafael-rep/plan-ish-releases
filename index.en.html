A plan that keeps up with your day

Arrive early or late and the rest of the day adjusts. You just decide.

Always know how the day is going

Where you are in the plan, how long is left and whether the rest still fits. The times that follow adjust themselves.

Ideas that fit on the way

Save places without a set time. With time to spare, Plan-ish suggests one on your way.

Search along the way

Coffee, fuel or a pharmacy, sorted by how much time each adds to the trip.

What to bring, stop by stop

Sunscreen goes with the viewpoint, boots with the hike. See the whole trip or one day.

Trip album

Photos by day and by stop, kept on your phone, and all exported in one file whenever you like.

Start however you like

From scratch, from a Community itinerary, or with the plan an AI assistant wrote for you.

At the wheel, just the essentials

Large type and one button to mark your arrival. When it is time to go, Google Maps opens with the next stop already set.

Travel with others, if you want

Shared plans

Edit the same plan at the same time, on a phone or in a browser. Everyone sees the others' changes live.

The Plan-ish Community

Trips from people who made them, confirmed by GPS. Copy one into your own planning and change what you like.

Only these two use a Community account. The rest of Plan-ish never needs one.
